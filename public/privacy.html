NutriLens privacy notice

Last updated: 10 August 2026

NutriLens is a small, invite-only nutrition journal. This notice explains the data used to provide the app.

Information the app uses

  • Your Google account email and display name for sign-in and access control.
  • Your nutrition targets, timezone, meals, notes, and estimated nutrition values.
  • Push-notification subscription details when you enable reminders.
  • Limited operational records for AI usage, reminder delivery, reliability, and abuse prevention.

Meal analysis

When you request an AI estimate, your meal description or a browser-compressed meal photo is sent securely to OpenAI for analysis. Meal photos are not saved in the NutriLens database. The resulting aggregate nutrition estimate is saved only after you confirm the meal.

Storage and sharing

Account and meal data is stored in Supabase and protected so signed-in users can access only their own records. Google provides sign-in, OpenAI provides optional meal analysis, and browser push services deliver reminders. NutriLens does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can use the local demo without creating an account, disable reminders, delete individual meals, or ask the app owner to remove your account and associated data. Avoid entering information you do not want processed by these services.

Health note. NutriLens provides estimates for personal tracking, not medical advice. Nutrition estimates can be inaccurate.
